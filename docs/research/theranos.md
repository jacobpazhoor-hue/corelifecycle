## The machine (mechanism)

| fact | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| Claimed test menu | 240+ tests from a single finger-stick drop | WSJ 2015-10-15 via TIME summary |
| Real Edison capability | By one former senior employee's account (cited by WSJ), only ~15 of 240+ tests were run on Edison as of late 2014; other reporting/trial testimony narrows this to essentially one reliable test — herpes simplex virus (HSV). Two different sourcing chains; use qualitatively ("a small handful, by some accounts just one"), not a single precise figure. | WSJ 2015-10-15; trial testimony summarized in secondary reporting |
| The workaround | Most of the test menu ran on modified, commercially available Siemens analyzers — built for a full venous draw, not a finger-prick volume — so finger-prick samples were diluted to generate enough volume | Fierce Biotech, Holmes trial admission coverage; Fortune trial coverage |
| Disclosure | Holmes testified the equipment modifications were disclosed only to the FDA, not to Walgreens, other partners, or investors — characterized internally as a trade secret | Fierce Biotech trial coverage |

## The reckoning

| fact | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| CMS sanctions | Letter dated ~March 18, 2016, reported April 13, 2016: proposed revocation of CLIA lab certificate and a 2-year ban on Holmes, Balwani and lab director Sunil Dhawan | Dark Daily; CNN Money 2016-04-13 |
| Tests voided | All Edison-generated 2014-2015 results voided/corrected, reported May 18, 2016; "tens of thousands" of corrected reports; in Arizona specifically, Theranos's own attorneys conceded more than 1 in 10 results given to Arizona patients were ultimately voided or corrected | TechCrunch 2016-05-18; Tucson.com |
| Arizona settlement | $4.6M refund to AZ customers + $200,000 civil penalty | Tucson.com |
| Forbes net worth revision | Cut from $4.5B to $0 on June 1, 2016, once the $9B valuation was judged unrealistic (Forbes estimated the company's realistic value at ~$800M, making Holmes's 50% stake worthless) | Forbes, Matthew Herper, 2016-06-01 |
| SEC charges | March 14, 2018: SEC charges Theranos, Holmes and Balwani with "massive fraud," raising $700M+ from investors on false/exaggerated claims | SEC press release 2018-41 |
| Holmes's SEC settlement | $500,000 civil penalty; barred from serving as officer/director of a public company for 10 years; relinquished voting control (converted Class B to Class A shares); returned 18.9M shares | SEC press release 2018-41 |
| Criminal indictment | June 14-15, 2018: federal grand jury indicts Holmes and Balwani, 2 counts conspiracy + 9 counts wire fraud (11 total); Holmes steps down as CEO same day | DOJ NDCA press release; NBC News |
| Dissolution | Sept 5, 2018: Theranos announces it will dissolve, remaining ~$5M cash to unsecured creditors; GC/CEO David Taylor: "we are now out of time" | NPR 2018-09-05; MIT Technology Review |
| Trial start | Aug 31, 2021, San Jose federal court, Judge Edward Davila | Wikipedia: Trial of Elizabeth Holmes |
| Verdict | Jan 3, 2022: guilty on 4 counts (1 conspiracy + 3 wire fraud, all investor-related); acquitted on all patient-related counts; jury deadlocked on 3 investor-related wire-fraud counts (mistrial on those) | CNN 2022-01-03; NBC News |
| Balwani's trial | Separate trial, verdict July 7, 2022: guilty on all 12 counts | CNBC 2022-07-07; CNN |
| Holmes sentencing | Nov 18, 2022: 135 months (11 years, 3 months) | NPR 2022-11-18; Forbes |
| Balwani sentencing | Dec 2022: ~155 months (nearly 13 years) — longer than Holmes | OPB 2022-12-07 |
| Holmes reports to prison | May 30, 2023, Federal Prison Camp Bryan, Texas | KBTX; CNN 2023-05-30 |

## CUT — considered and left out because I could not verify to this standard

- The exact precise number of Edison-reliable tests (accounts differ: "15 of 240+" vs. "just one, herpes"). The script uses both framings rather than picking a single unverifiable number.
- Any specific individual patient's test result or medical outcome — no single patient case was verified to primary-source standard in this pass; the script uses the general, sourced Arizona "1 in 10" figure and the "tens of thousands voided" figure instead.
- Individual join dates for Kissinger, Mattis, Perry and Nunn on the Board of Counselors — only "recruited by Shultz starting 2011" is confirmed.
- The claim "she posed as a deaf-mute" or similar embellishments — not applicable to this subject; noted only to confirm no invented incident was substituted in.
- A precise, single total-funding figure — "$945M lifetime" and "$700M+ during the fraud period" are both real but describe different scopes; the script uses the SEC's $700M+ figure since it is tied to a specific, sourced claim.
- Any quote attributed to John Carreyrou reacting to the sentencing ("she didn't apologize to patients...") — found only in secondary aggregation, not confirmed to a primary transcript. Not used.
- The superseding indictment's exact count total and the mid-trial dismissal of one wire-fraud count — the script uses the original, well-documented "eleven counts" from the June 2018 indictment rather than the more disputed later count.
