# Research: Ottoman Empire — "Your Life as Every Rank in the Ottoman Empire"

Format: `history`. Level labels by rank/era, not job title. The episode compresses the **devshirme
("kul") system** — the empire's real, documented pipeline that took enslaved Christian boys from the
Balkans to the second-highest office in the state — into one composite POV climb, the same
mythic-compression the ROMAN/SAMURAI/MONGOL/GLADIATOR history-format packs use. All hard facts below
are web-verified 2026-08-06; sources noted inline. Where a per-rank number has no surviving payroll
record, the spine uses documented UNITS OF VALUE (akçe wages, timar/hass revenue bands) and is
flagged ILLUSTRATIVE.

## The real ladder (composite, elaborated to 8 rungs for pacing — flagged)

No single historical person literally held all 8 rungs (a devshirme boy went EITHER the Janissary
military track OR the Enderun palace-school track, not always through a Janissary command first) —
this episode compresses the two real tracks into one composite career, the same compression the other
history episodes use.

1. **Balkan peasant boy** — pre-devshirme village life, Christian family, Ottoman Balkans (any era
   c. 1450–1600, the system's peak centuries).
2. **Acemi oğlan** ("novice boy") — VERIFIED: collected via devshirme, the empire's child levy —
   roughly one boy per 40 households, ages ~8–20 (most sources cluster 8–18), taken every few years,
   inspected, converted to Islam, sent to Anatolian Turkish families to learn the language/customs
   before formal training. [osmanlitarihi.tr; realmofhistory.com; timelessmyths.com]
3. **Janissary** (yeni çeri, "new soldier") — VERIFIED: first campaign; entry wage **3 akçe/day**
   (Ahmet I-era rate) + 12 akçe/quarter clothing allowance + 30 akçe weapons allowance; forbidden to
   marry or grow a beard (mustache only) — the corps was legally family. [military-history.fandom.com;
   Wikipedia "Janissary"]
4. **Çorbacı** ("soup-maker" — the literal historical title for an orta/regiment's colonel) — command
   of ~100+ men; VERIFIED cavalry-track promotion carried a minimum **10 akçe/day**. Janissary officer
   titles are literally kitchen ranks (çorbacı=soup-maker, aşçıbaşı=head cook, karakullukçu=scullion) —
   the corps' whole identity organized around the regimental cauldron (**kazan-ı şerif**, the "noble
   cauldron"), and overturning it was the formal signal of mutiny. [military-history.fandom.com;
   qalam.global; hurriyetdailynews.com]
5. **Sanjakbey** (district governor) — promoted out of the corps into administration, granted a
   **ziamet** (mid-tier revenue grant, VERIFIED range **20,000–99,999 akçe/year**), commanding timar-
   holding cavalry (sipahis) owed for service. [Wikipedia "Timar"; kureansiklopedi.com]
6. **Beylerbey / Pasha** (provincial governor-general) — VERIFIED: granted a **hass** (revenue over
   **100,000 akçe/year**), the same tier as viziers and defterdars; entitled to the **three horsetails
   (tuğ)** standard. [Wikipedia "Timar"]
7. **Vizier** — a seated member of the **Imperial Council (Divan-ı Hümayun)** at Topkapi; the Divan's
   inner ring of viziers was a small, single-digit number in any given era (varies by sultan/reform —
   flagged illustrative, not a fixed universal count).
8. **Grand Vizier (Sadrazam)** — VERIFIED: held the sultan's personal seal, could command armies in
   the field as **Serdar-i Ekrem**, was in practice the empire's day-to-day ruler — a former slave
   outranking every free-born Ottoman except the dynasty itself. VERIFIED cost: across the empire's
   ~600-year history, **284 men held the office; 44 were executed on the sultan's direct order, and 11
   more died in rebellions** (≈1 in 6 by direct execution alone). [hurriyetdailynews.com; Quora/sourced
   aggregation of the standard 284/44 figures repeated across Ottoman-history secondary sources]

## Verified facts used as story grounding

- **Devshirme ("collection")**: a blood-tax levy of Christian boys from the Balkans, ~1 per 40
  households, ages roughly 8–20; smartest boys routed to the Enderun palace school (future
  administrators/viziers), the rest to Acemi Oğlan barracks and the Janissary corps. Devshirme boys
  were legally **kul** — slaves of the sultan personally — which is the episode's central irony: the
  empire's highest non-dynastic office was, by law, filled by an enslaved person. [osmanlitarihi.tr;
  realmofhistory.com]
- **The kazan (cauldron) culture**: every barracks orta had a ceremonial soup cauldron; Janissary caps
  were ornamented with a spoon; refusing the sultan's food signaled discontent, and **physically
  overturning the cauldron was the literal declaration of mutiny**. This is not metaphor — it is
  standing military ritual, documented across multiple 17th–19th century incidents. **SHARE-WORTHY
  BEAT #1**: in 1622, Janissaries overturned the cauldron and deposed AND executed Sultan Osman II,
  age 17 — the first Ottoman sultan ever killed by his own soldiers. In 1826 the same ritual opened
  the "Auspicious Incident," when Sultan Mahmud II finally destroyed the corps outright.
  [hurriyetdailynews.com/opinion; grokipedia.com "Auspicious Incident"]
- **The Tower of Justice grille**: VERIFIED — a latticed window (**Kafes-i Müşebbek**) in the Tower of
  Justice looks directly down into the Divan chamber; the sultan (and at times the **Valide Sultan**,
  the sultan's mother) could watch council sessions unseen. No vizier ever knew, in the moment, whether
  the throne was listening. **SHARE-WORTHY BEAT #2** (used as this episode's "wait — that's real?"
  moment). [topkapipalace.com.tr, "The Secret of the Tower of Justice"]
- **Execution without blood**: VERIFIED — spilling royal or high-official blood was taboo, so viziers
  and dynasty members were strangled instead (silk cord, bowstring, or bare hands), traditionally by
  the **bostancıbaşı** (head gardener), who commanded a ~5,000-strong corps that doubled as palace
  guard/executioners. The **44-of-284 Grand Vizier execution figure** above is this episode's apex-cost
  number. [listverse.com; smithsonianmag.com]
- **Law of fratricide**: VERIFIED — codified by Mehmed II ("acceptable for any of my descendants... for
  the common benefit of the people"); a new sultan's brothers (and their male line) could be lawfully
  strangled to remove rival claimants. Referenced as ambient dread, not depicted as the protagonist's
  own arc (the protagonist is kul, not dynasty). [belleten.gov.tr; dailysabah.com]
- **Corps growth**: VERIFIED — Janissary numbers: ~1,000 (1400) → 7,841 (1484) → 13,599 (1574) →
  37,627 (1609) → ~135,000 (1826, just before abolition). Used as ambient scale, not a per-scene
  number. [Wikipedia "Ottoman military reforms"]
- **Sokollu Mehmed Pasha** (Grand Vizier 1565–1579, a former devshirme boy from Bosnia): the high-water
  mark of the office's power — assassinated at his own public audience by a disguised petitioner with a
  concealed dagger, Oct 11, 1579. Referenced as background/grounding only — not fictionalized into the
  protagonist's personal arc. [Wikipedia "Sokollu Mehmed Pasha"]

## The $ / stakes escalation spine (akçe-denominated where verified; flagged illustrative elsewhere)

1. Peasant boy: **1 IN 40** — households, the fraction that pays in sons, not silver.
2. Acemi oğlan: taken from the count; no wage yet — the cost is paid before any money starts.
3. Janissary: **3 AKÇE/DAY** — the standard entry wage, verified (Ahmet I-era rate), plus 12 akçe/
   quarter clothing + 30 akçe weapons.
4. Çorbacı: **10 AKÇE/DAY** — the verified cavalry-track promotion floor; command of an orta.
5. Sanjakbey: **20,000–99,999 AKÇE/YEAR** (ziamet band) — a district's revenue passes through your
   hands; none of it is legally "yours" the way a wage is.
6. Beylerbey/Pasha: **100,000+ AKÇE/YEAR** (hass band) — a province, three horsetails.
7. Vizier: a seat on the Divan — ILLUSTRATIVE, no single verified per-vizier stipend figure used
   on-screen; framed instead by the Tower of Justice grille (the real, verified surveillance detail).
8. Grand Vizier: the sultan's seal, command of armies — undercut by the verified apex-cost number,
   **44 OF 284** Grand Vizierss executed by direct sultan's order across Ottoman history.

## Characters (fictional composite — no real historical figure is depicted as the protagonist)

- **You** — unnamed POV protagonist, a composite Balkan peasant boy taken by the devshirme.
- **Davud Ağa** — mentor, an aging çorbacı who claims you into his orta, teaches the corps' code
  ("the kazan is your mother now"). Dies at the midpoint reversal — killed in the chaos of the 1622
  cauldron mutiny that deposes Sultan Osman II, torn between the oath he swore you and the mutiny he
  can't stop.
- **Yusuf** — rival acemi oğlan taken in the same devshirme convoy, faster and colder; rises alongside
  you, later a rival pasha you must outmaneuver for the Grand Vizierate.
- Real historical figures (Mehmed II, Osman II, Sokollu Mehmed Pasha, Mahmud II) are referenced only
  as background/grounding for independently documented events — never given invented dialogue or a
  fictionalized personal arc.
